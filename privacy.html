Privacy Policy

Taxi and Fly · Last updated 3 November 2026

Taxi and Fly is a booking app that connects passengers with licensed taxi drivers for trips between Athens and Athens International Airport.

Data we collect

  • Booking details you type in the form: name, phone number, pickup point, date, time, number of passengers. You may book for someone else; the form is not filled from your account.
  • Sign-in email from Google (Android) or Apple (iPhone), once after you accept the terms. We store this email on our server with the device identifier so the same phone does not have to sign in again. We do not receive or store your password.
  • Device identifier and push token so we can send booking and chat notifications.
  • Approximate location only while you use the app, to suggest a pickup point. We do not track you in the background.

Why we use it

To send your booking to nearby drivers, to operate the shared-ride option, to contact you about the trip, and to keep the service safe (one sign-in per device, linked to email).

Who sees it

Licensed drivers who receive your request see the booking details needed for the trip. We do not sell personal data. Payment is made directly to the driver in the vehicle; we do not process card payments in the app.

How long we keep it

Booking and account records are kept as long as needed to provide the service and to handle support or legal requests, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can delete your data from inside the app (Delete my data). You may also email us. We will erase your bookings, terms acceptance, device sign-in record and related account data that we hold.
